# Ergonomic Controls

Shortcuts that fit your hands, and a browser that stays out of the game.

## Features

1. Control Profiles, in the module settings: keyboard, mouse and controller maps with every shortcut colored by reach, from where you play: one hand on your movement keys (WASD, arrows, numpad…) and the other on the mouse.
2. Each shortcut tells what it costs (hand switch, stretch, keys at once, same key as another action) and suggests free keys nearby.
3. Filters for out of reach, shared key and stone keys. Stone keys are keys that already have an owner: your movement keys, the left and right mouse buttons, and the browser and system shortcuts, shown with their default keys; you can edit them to match your browser if you change them (just visuals).
4. Keys used by several actions are outlined, and clicking a key lists everything that uses it.
5. Combo keys: tap twice or more, hold for a time, press keys together or in sequence. The key alone keeps working and only waits when its action fires once per press.
7. As many profiles as you want: create, duplicate, rename, export, import or reset to Foundry's defaults.
8. Turn off unused browser keys while foundry is on your screen: back and forward (side buttons, Alt+←/→, swipe), middle-button autoscroll, save, print, bookmarks, history, downloads, address bar and browser menu. Ctrl+W, Ctrl+T, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+1–9 and the like don't close, open or switch the tab.
